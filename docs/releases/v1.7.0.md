# Darkbloom Control v1.7.0

- Organize Models into collapsible Enabled and Available groups, with narrower
  cards and up to two columns. Models that still need downloading are visually muted.
- Add an independent daily-runtime slider to each model card. Forecasts are
  what-if estimates based on observed activity and configured electricity cost;
  they do not schedule or change provider runtime. Earnings inputs are account-level
  and assume this Mac produced the recorded work for that model.
- Show whole-Mac GPU utilization as a menu-bar ring, with temperature coloring
  when fresh readings are available. Missing or stale utilization omits the ring;
  missing temperature uses a neutral color.
- Share GPU sampling between the menu bar and dashboard to avoid duplicate polling.

Coming soon: the iOS companion and remote pairing/control. Fan controls, richer
statistics, and automatic model warming also remain planned and are not enabled
in this release.
